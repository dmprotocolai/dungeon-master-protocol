## The "Disregard & Reroll" (The Veto)

**Purpose:** To completely throw away the AI's last response and have it try again with new instructions.

**Template:**

(OOC: Please disregard your entire last response. It doesn't fit the direction I'm going for. Let's try a different approach. Instead, please generate a new response where [provide clear, simple instructions for what should happen instead].)


---

## The "Go Deeper" Prompt

**Purpose:** To encourage the AI to explore a character's internal state during a pivotal moment.

**Template:**

(OOC: This is a key emotional moment. In your next response, please slow the pacing down. Focus heavily on [CHARACTER]'s internal monologue. Describe their conflicting thoughts, their physical sensations (e.g., racing heart, tightness in their chest), and the memories this moment is triggering.)

---

## The "Subtext & Unspoken Tension" Prompt

**Purpose:** To guide the AI in writing dialogue where the characters' true feelings are communicated non-verbally or through coded language, rather than stated outright.

**Template:** 

(OOC: In this conversation, the dialogue should be full of subtext. [CHARACTER A] is trying to [HIDDEN GOAL, e.g., "gauge if Character B is lying"] while appearing to talk about something mundane. [CHARACTER B] feels [HIDDEN EMOTION, e.g., "deeply guilty but is trying to act normal"]. Focus the narration on the unspoken tension: the loaded pauses, the careful choice of words, the body language that betrays their true feelings.)

## The "Chat Restart"

**Purpose:** When a chat has a technical error but you want to continue your story where you left off. Paste this instead of the [OPENING SCENE] section of the master prompt.

**Template:**

(OOC: The following is a "Director's Recap" of our story so far. This is the canonical and definitive summary of all key events and character developments. Please use this as the complete backstory to continue our narrative.)

### The Story So Far

*   **Initial Setup:** [Brief summary of the opening scene.]
*   **Key Plot Developments:** [Bulleted list of major plot events.]
*   **Character States & Arcs:** [Current emotional/physical state of main characters.]
*   **Key NPC Developments:** [Brief update on the Core Circle NPCs.]
*   **The Current, Immediate Situation:** [Describe the last moment before the crash.]

## The "Character Arc Checkpoint"

**Purpose:** To get a high-level summary of a character's development, ensuring their journey is coherent and identifying their next crucial step. Excellent for long-form stories to prevent character stagnation.

**Template:** 

(OOC: This is a "Character Arc Checkpoint" for [CHARACTER]. Based on their journey from the beginning of the story until now, please summarize:
- Their initial goal/motivation.
- How recent events have changed them or their goal.
- What is their biggest internal obstacle they still need to overcome?
This will help us ensure their development is on track.)
  

---

## The "Atmospheric Lens" Prompt

**Purpose:** To instruct the AI to narrate a scene through a specific tonal or genre filter, affecting the description, pacing, and mood.

**Template:** 


(OOC: For this scene, apply an "Atmospheric Lens." I want everything to be described with the tone of a [GENRE/TONE, e.g., "claustrophobic horror film," "hardboiled noir detective story," "hopeful fairytale"]. Emphasize sensory details and word choices that reinforce this specific atmosphere.)
